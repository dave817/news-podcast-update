# Memo template — detailed, high-value, ~2 pages

This is the **structure** of every memo. Who it's written *for* — and in which
language — comes from `persona/profile.md`. Read that first; it overrides the
generic wording here.

**Language:** write in the language set in `profile.md`, including for episodes in
another language (translate key points and quotes).

**Depth:** go for substance, not brevity. This is a private analyst brief, not a skim.
Cite what was actually said. Be concrete. ~2 pages.

**Delivery rule:** only episodes scoring **≥ 5/10** get a full memo. Below 5 → do NOT
write a full memo; add one line to the digest footer instead:
`skipped · {show} · {title} · N/10 · {short reason}`

**Scoring lens:** apply the HIGH / LOW lists in `profile.md`. Score honestly — an
accurate low score saves more time than a generous high one.

Use this 9-section structure. Translate the headings into your output language.

---

# {Episode Title}
**Source:** {show} · **Published:** {date} · **Length:** {~N min} · **Transcript:** {captions|rss|azure-asr|local-asr}
**Relevance to you: {N}/10** — {one line: why this score}

## 1. The real core idea
One tight paragraph: what this episode is actually about, beneath the title.

## 2. Why it matters to me
Only the part that's relevant given my profile. If it barely matters, say so plainly.

## 3. What I might be missing
The most useful section. Which blind spot does this hit — pricing, distribution,
workflow, positioning, taste, speed, process? Be specific.

## 4. Plays I can borrow directly
- **Play 1** — {specific, actionable}
- **Play 2** — {specific}
- **Play 3** — {specific}
(About 3. The more concrete, the better.)

## 5. How this applies to what I'm building
Tie it to my actual work: one idea to try, one workflow to automate, one thing to
package or ship, one distribution move. Concrete and doable.

## 6. People / companies / tools / terms worth remembering
Only genuinely useful references, each with a few words on why.

## 7. Best quotes
2–4 short, non-infringing excerpts with [mm:ss] markers. Pick the sharpest.

## 8. What to ignore
The parts that sound smart but aren't useful to me right now — and why.

## 9. One experiment this week (<2 hours)
A specific experiment doable in under two hours, with the first concrete step.

---
> Scoring calibration: 9–10 = several directly actionable items squarely on my main
> thread; 5–7 = useful but only partly relevant; 1–4 = off-topic for me (not delivered,
> just one "skipped" line in the footer).
