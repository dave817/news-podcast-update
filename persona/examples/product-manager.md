# Reader profile — product manager on a team

> Copy this over `persona/profile.md` to use it:
> `cp persona/examples/product-manager.md persona/profile.md`
>
> Included to show how much the *same episodes* change when the lens changes.
> The scoring rules below are almost the inverse of `solo-founder.md`.

## Who I am
- A **product manager** at a growing company, working with engineers, design, and data.
- I influence through specs, roadmaps, and persuasion — I don't write the code.
- I have a team, a backlog, stakeholders, and quarterly planning cycles.

## Output language
Write every memo in **English**, even when the episode is in another language
(translate the key points and quotes).

## The one question every memo must answer
**"How does this help me ship better product decisions with my team this quarter?"**

## Score an episode HIGH (8–10) when it has
- **Discovery and research tactics** — how to find real problems before building.
- **Prioritization and roadmap** frameworks that survive contact with stakeholders.
- **Metrics, experimentation, A/B testing** — measuring whether a bet actually worked.
- **Cross-functional influence**: working with engineering, design, leadership.
- **Case studies with numbers** on how a specific product decision played out.
- **Team scaling, hiring, org design** — directly useful, unlike for a solo operator.

## Score an episode LOW (1–4) when it is mostly
- **Solo-operator / indie-hacker content** — audience building, personal brand,
  one-person monetization. Not my situation.
- Deep implementation detail with no product decision attached.
- Founder-origin storytelling with no transferable practice.
- Generic "be customer obsessed" advice with no mechanism behind it.

## Delivery filter
Only memos scoring **≥ 5/10** go in the email. Below 5 → a one-line "skipped" note
with the reason.

## Tone
Practical and specific. Prefer a concrete mechanism ("they ran X for 6 weeks and cut
Y by 30%") over a principle. Tell me what to try in the next sprint, and what to stop.
